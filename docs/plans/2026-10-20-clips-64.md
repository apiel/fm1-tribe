# 64 clips per track, the clip menu, song mode

## Goal

From NOTE.md ("Clip page"): more than 16 clips per track (64 now, 128 later), a way to save the clip
on screen into a specific clip, to remove a clip, and song mode (clips played one after another, an
empty clip ends the loop).

There is no separate copy buffer. "Copy" is the clip that is loaded on the track, including unsaved
edits: the clip menu writes it into the slot you pick.

## Status

- Option A (flash-backed clips): done. Only each track's playing clip is in RAM, plus three prepared copies per track
  (`clip_prep`). The other clips are read from the log when they are needed. Song mode queues the next clip from the main
  loop at each pattern start (`song_tick`), a pattern before the switch. The grid reads a has-steps bit per clip. The
  playing clip's unsaved edits are lost when its track launches another clip (shown on screen, and in the manual).
  A track resumes on the clip it was on at the last settings save. The device build fits: RAM 96128 of 98304 B,
  pool 304456 of 344064 B.
- Phase 3 (song mode): done. KNOB 4 on the CLIPS page (OFF / LOOP / ONE SHOT), stored per track in the settings. The
  settings record grew by ten bytes; a record from before is read as a prefix, so saved settings still load
  (`tests/scenarios/song_save1.tribe` and `song_save2.tribe`). `tests/scenarios/song.tribe` checks LOOP and ONE SHOT.
  ONE SHOT on the last clip of the bank (index 63) has no empty clip after it, so the track stays on it.
- Phase 2 (the clip menu): done. `tests/scenarios/clip_menu.tribe` covers copy (with an unsaved step), delete, leaving the
  menu with another button, and refusing SAVE on the loaded clip. Clip keys launch on release.
- Phase 1 (64 clips, banks): done. 35 host tests pass, including the bank scenario (`tests/scenarios/clips64.tribe`),
  the round trip from the released build's log (`tests/fixtures/v3_clip_log.bin`) and the store test with every clip full.
- Changed from the first draft of phase 1: the log grows to 69 sectors (the most before OBJ_PANEL at 0xDC000), records
  are 396 bytes, and a sector is no longer tied to the one being written. A write goes in the free tail of any partly
  written sector. Before each write the log keeps two sectors' worth of free slots, so cleaning always has room; if it
  cannot, the write fails with -1 and the log is left as it was. With 641 records in 690 slots, cleaning one sector at a
  time was not enough (the store test found this).
- Old logs (version 3, 512-byte records) are converted once at boot: the newest record of each clip and of the settings
  is read, the old sectors are erased and the clips are written in today's layout. A power cut during that one-time
  conversion can lose the clips that were not yet written; the RAM copy is the only other copy.
- Not checked on the device: the RAM pool with 64 clips (233 KB of `proj`), and the device build. The JIELI toolchain is
  not installed here.

## Budgets (from the code, 2026-10-20)

- `clip_t` is 364 bytes. 10 tracks x 64 clips = 233 KB in RAM, inside the 336 KB pool that holds `proj`
  (`firmware/src/tribe/engine.c:12`). The rest of the pool (effect buffers) must be measured on a device
  build, since the JIELI toolchain is not available here.
- The clip log is `PLAT_LOG_SECTORS` = 48 sectors (192 KB), one 512-byte record per clip, 8 per sector.
  64 clips per track need 640 records (80 sectors), which do not fit. Records must be packed: 396 bytes
  (32-byte header + 364) gives 10 per sector, about 253 KB for 640 clips, within the ~292 KB free in the
  data area (`0x97000..0xDFFFF`), with `0x9D000` and `0xDE000` kept for other objects.
- 128 clips per track is not in this plan: 507 KB raw does not fit in flash or RAM. It needs clips
  stored in flash and loaded when queued, plus compressed records (`firmware/src/app/lz.c`). Separate plan.

## Phase 1: 64 clips, four banks of 16

- `NCLIP` 16 -> 64 (`firmware/src/tribe/tribe.h:12`). `NKEY` in `store.c` follows.
- Records packed to 396 bytes and 10 per sector, so 640 clips fit in the log. Bump `REC_VER`. Each record
  names its track and clip in the header, so old saves load into the new layout by those fields, not by
  offset. Test with `tests/fixtures/v1_store.bin` and `v0102_store.bin`.
- Banks: `ui.bank` (0..3) selects which 16 clips the keys show. The CLIPS page draws the bank's 16 cells
  and a `BANK n/4` label. A key launches `bank * 16 + key`.
- Bank change: OCT- / OCT+ on the HOME page (they already change step pages on SEQ).

Tests: `tests/host/store_test.c` runs with 64 clips; the power-cut test covers the new record size.

## Phase 2: the clip menu (long press on a clip key)

Opening: press and hold a clip key on the HOME page for about 400 ms (the timer SEQ's config page uses).
The clip under the finger is the target. The menu shows its number and three rows:

| Row | What it does | Screen line while it is highlighted |
|---|---|---|
| SAVE | copies the track's loaded clip (with unsaved edits) into the target, and saves the target to flash | `SAVE CLIP 3 <- CLIP 1` and `PRESS SAVE`; `OVERWRITE` when the target has content |
| DELETE | empties the target (no steps; it becomes the end of a song chain) and saves it to flash | `DELETE CLIP 3` and `PRESS SAVE` |
| EXIT | closes the menu | `EXIT` |

Controls:
- **KNOB 1** moves the highlight between SAVE, DELETE and EXIT.
- **SAVE button** (the hardware one) confirms the highlighted row. For EXIT it just closes.
- **Any other button** exits without doing anything.
- Releasing the clip key does not count as a choice.

Rules:
- SAVE on the loaded clip itself is refused: `SAME CLIP`.
- Both SAVE and DELETE write to flash at once. The SAVE button press is the one confirmation, so no
  second "overwrite?" step.
- The track stays on the loaded clip afterwards. DELETE on the clip that is playing makes the track go
  silent at once.
- Clip keys launch on release instead of press, so a long press does not also launch the clip. A short
  tap still launches as before.
- Works on the HOME page only, where the clip keys are.

## Phase 3: song mode

- A per-track param on the CLIPS page, **KNOB 4**: `OFF / LOOP / ONE SHOT`. KNOB 1 is the track's level
  and KNOB 2 and 3 stay free. (I read "4th param" as KNOB 4, next to the others on that page.)
- Stored in the settings (`dsettings_t`), so it's a settings-record change with a migration: old projects
  load with every track at OFF.
- A chain is the clips in index order from the playing one: clip `i`, then `i+1`, and so on. The first
  empty clip ends the chain. LOOP starts again at the first clip of the run; ONE SHOT stops after the last.
- Playback uses the existing end-of-pattern launch (`dk_launch`). Each step of the chain queues the next
  clip. Chains cross banks, so a chain of 20 clips starts on bank 1 and continues on bank 2.
- Tests in `tests/host/seq_test.c`: chain order, the empty-clip end, LOOP wrap, ONE SHOT stop.

## Not in this plan

- HOME long press: no function for now (its tap is unchanged).
- Paste to another track: dropped unless you ask for it. The menu copies from the track's own loaded clip.
- 128 clips per track (flash-backed clips and compressed records).

## Decisions

1. Clip keys launch on release, so the long press can open the menu (needed for this design).
2. SAVE and DELETE write flash at once, with the SAVE button as the only confirmation (as written above).
3. Deleting the clip that is playing: silent at once (allowed).
